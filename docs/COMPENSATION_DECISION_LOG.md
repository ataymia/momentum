## 2026-10-09 manager-vacancy clarification

Confirmed by operations: the approved representative rate remains 2.50%. The 0.50% override is payable **only** to the eligible, officially designated Sales Manager who holds the Sales Manager role and job title. Neither a full-permission Administrator nor an owner acting as an interim manager receives this manager commission by virtue of administrative authority.

While the position is vacant, the otherwise illustrative 0.50% is retained by Momentum. It is **not** an employee bonus, commission liability, owner distribution, or automatically booked net profit. Management reporting may disclose this retained amount separately without generating a payable. If a Sales Manager is appointed, eligibility must use historically verified assignment on each new qualifying earning; do not backfill prior vacant-period amounts as owed to the new manager.

The 2.50% and 0.50% both apply to *Qualifying Net Collected Sales*, not simply paid gross invoices. The effective date remains missing, and percentage commission must not enter payroll without a written prospective effective date and reconciled qualifying-sales evidence.

The fixed opening/sustained account bonuses remain independent of these percentages.
